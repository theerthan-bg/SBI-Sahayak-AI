# SBI-Sahayak-AI
AI-powered multilingual banking assistant for digital adoption, fraud protection, and voice-guided banking support for SBI customers.





# 🚀 Overview

- SBI Sahayak AI is an AI-powered multilingual banking assistant designed to improve digital banking adoption among SBI customers.

- The platform helps users perform banking activities confidently through voice assistance, fraud detection, scam alerts, and step-by-step digital banking guidance.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

- Theme: Agentic AI & Emerging Tech

- Problem Statement: Digital Adoption

- Many customers, especially senior citizens and rural users, face challenges while using digital banking services due to language barriers, lack of digital literacy, and fear of fraud.

---

# 💡 Solution

- SBI Sahayak AI acts as a smart banking companion that:

- Provides voice-based banking guidance
- Supports Kannada, Hindi, and English
- Detects potential fraud and scam messages
- Educates users on digital banking services
- Assists users in UPI and YONO operations

---

# ✨ Key Features

- AI Banking Coach

- Step-by-step guidance for digital banking activities.

- AI Scam Shield

- Detects suspicious links, messages, and fraud attempts.

- Multilingual Support

- Supports regional languages for better accessibility.

- Senior Citizen Mode

- Simple UI and voice-first experience.

---

# 🏗 Architecture

- Customer → Voice/Text Input → AI Engine → Fraud Detection + Banking Knowledge Base → Response Generator → Customer
